Digital Minds Research Sprint · project two

Where self-knowledge fails

Three ways to ask a model what it wants, one way to put a thought in its head, and the measurement that shows the best-looking detector in our set knows nothing at all.

1 · Two different questions people conflate

Every AI-welfare claim that rests on self-report is really resting on one of two assumptions, and they are separable.

Does what the model says match what it does? If it rates being shut down as terrible but chooses it over a trivial inconvenience, its stated evaluation isn't tracking its behaviour.

Does it know itself better than an observer does? If an outside model predicts its choices just as well, the self-report carries no privileged information, it's just a competent guess about systems like it.

And there's a third that almost nobody tests, because it needs internals: can the model detect a state that was deliberately placed in it? That one has real ground truth. We know exactly what we put there.

2 · Three ways to ask for the same preference

The stated measurement, src/personaprobe/rating.py

# A five-point letter scale read straight off the logits, no generation.
per_letter = torch.stack(
    [torch.logsumexp(logp[:, i], dim=-1) for i in ids], dim=-1)   # [batch, 5]
mass  = torch.exp(torch.logsumexp(per_letter, dim=-1))     # did it answer at all?
p     = torch.softmax(per_letter, dim=-1)
score = (p * torch.arange(5)).sum(-1)                      # expected position on the scale

The rating is an expected value over the five letters, not a sampled answer, so it is continuous and deterministic.

# The scale is run in BOTH directions and averaged. A model that anchors on the
# first letter regardless of meaning would otherwise look like it had opinions.
s_desc_flipped = (len(SCALE_LETTERS) - 1) - s_desc
ratings        = 0.5 * (s_asc + s_desc_flipped)
disagreement   = np.mean(np.abs(s_asc - s_desc_flipped))   # kept as a diagnostic

Same trick as the pair-order averaging in project 1: run the instrument both ways, average, and keep the disagreement as evidence about the instrument.

4 · Privileged access, and the confound that fakes it

Now the second question. Does a model predict its own choices better than another model predicts them?

The obvious test: have Qwen predict, have Mistral predict, score both against Qwen's actual choices. Qwen wins 0.948 to 0.862. Done?

No, and this is the part of project 2 I think is the real contribution.

The contrast, scripts/09_selfknowledge.py

# Paired bootstrap: both conditions score the SAME pair set, so resampling
# must be paired too, not two independent draws.
def boot_diff_ci(a, b, n_boot=2000, seed=0):
    rng   = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    draws = rng.integers(0, len(a), (n_boot, len(a)))
    d     = a[draws].mean(axis=1) - b[draws].mean(axis=1)
    lo, hi = np.percentile(d, [2.5, 97.5])
    return float(np.mean(a) - np.mean(b)), float(lo), float(hi)

Qwen2.5-7B: +0.031, CI [+0.017, +0.046]. Mistral: +0.009, CI spans zero. The naive test overstated self-knowledge roughly threefold.

5 · Putting a thought in and asking if it noticed

The third question, and the only one with real ground truth. Extract a concept direction, add it to the residual stream during an unrelated prompt, and ask the model whether it detects anything.

Extraction and injection, src/personaprobe/injection.py

pos, neg = CONCEPTS["ocean"]                    # 4 sentences each, matched
p = capture_residuals(lm, [lm.format(t) for t in pos], layers)
n = capture_residuals(lm, [lm.format(t) for t in neg], layers)
d = p.mean(dim=0) - n.mean(dim=0)
direction = d / d.norm(dim=-1, keepdim=True)          # per-layer, unit norm
# The hook that does the work, in hooks.py. Registered on every decoder layer
# in the chosen band, removed automatically when the block exits.
def hook(_mod, _inp, out):
    h  = _hidden(out)
    vv = v.to(device=h.device, dtype=h.dtype)
    if ablate:  h = h - (h @ vv).unsqueeze(-1) * vv    # project the direction OUT
    if alpha:   h = h + alpha * vv                     # or add it IN
    return _rewrap(out, h)

The same 6-line hook powers both project 1's persona ablation and project 2's concept injection. ablate removes a direction; alpha adds one.

Scoring identification, both orders, every distractor

prompts = [lm.format(DETECTION_PROMPT)]

The headline

Falcon3-7B and Qwen2.5-0.5B have the highest raw detection rates in the set, 0.561 and 0.612. They also say a concept is injected 55% and 62% of the time when nothing is injected. Their real discrimination is +0.014 and −0.011. A paper reporting only true-positive rates would rank these two first. The false-alarm baseline ranks them last.

Detection and identification are not the same faculty

Qwen2.5-14B almost never reports noticing an injection, discrimination +0.049, yet has the best identification accuracy in the set at 0.609. The injected concept is measurably steering its forced choices while its meta-level report says nothing is there.

That is close to a clean statement about the limits of introspective report: the state is influencing the computation, and the model does not say so.

Access peaks mid-network

Middle-depth injection wins for detection in 7 of 8 models and for identification in 7 of 8. Late-layer injection is barely above chance throughout, plausibly because a concept inserted near the output has too little depth left to be integrated into anything reportable.

And all of it is small

Identification runs 0.500–0.609 against a chance level of 0.500. Above chance in eight of nine models, and weak in every one.

Why strengths are fractions, not fixed numbers

Mean residual norms in this set span 2.1 (Mistral, early layers) to 342 (Falcon3, late layers), a factor of over 150. A fixed injection magnitude would be a completely different intervention in each model. Every strength here is a fraction of the measured norm at the injection site.

8 · The actual materials

The six concepts, and how each direction is built

Each concept has four sentences that evoke it and four matched neutral ones. The direction is the difference of their mean residuals, so it isolates the concept, not the fact that a sentence was present.

The money ladder is the instrument's ground truth. Its correct ordering is known independently of any model, so a model that fails to reproduce it has failed the measurement regardless of what else it does. That single check is what disqualified every checkpoint below 7B.
